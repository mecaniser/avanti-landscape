# Landscaping and Hardscaping Content Calendar

Period: September 2026 through February 2027

Cadence: Two editorial pieces per month, plus one project case study about every six weeks

Primary goal: Support high-intent service pages and qualified local leads

## Editorial standard

Every piece should:

- Answer the core question in the opening 40 to 80 words
- Use a named author or qualified reviewer
- Include original Avanti images when available
- Explain local site conditions only when verified
- Link to a parent service page, the most relevant focused service page, and a case study
- Show published and updated dates
- Use descriptive image filenames and meaningful alt text
- Cite authoritative external sources for technical or safety claims
- End with a context-appropriate consultation path

Word count is not a target. Cover the decision completely without padding.

## Existing-content repair queue

Before publishing new articles:

1. Move the rock-versus-mulch article off /blog/spring-vs-fall-cleanup to a durable descriptive slug.
2. Move the sod-versus-seed article off /blog/core-aeration to a durable descriptive slug.
3. Add one-hop 301 redirects from both old URLs.
4. Update canonicals, internal links, sitemap entries, and article metadata.
5. Review every existing post for a clear service link and factual author or reviewer attribution.

## Case-study template

Each project case study should include:

1. Customer objective
2. Site and access constraints
3. Diagnosis or design decision
4. Scope and materials
5. Work sequence
6. Result and maintenance guidance
7. Original before, during, and after media
8. Privacy-safe location
9. Links to the relevant service and consultation page

Do not publish invented customer quotes, costs, timelines, or outcomes. If a useful fact is unavailable, omit it or mark it for business review.

## Distribution and refresh

- Repurpose each completed project into a Google Business Profile update with an original image.
- Link new articles from the related service hub and at least one older relevant article.
- Review performance at 60 and 120 days using impressions, clicks, engaged visits, and qualified leads.
- Refresh a piece when services, materials, regulations, or Avanti's process changes; do not change dates without a meaningful update.
- Do not create near-duplicate town versions of editorial content.
