# Avanti Landscaping SEO Strategy

Prepared: August 19, 2026

Reconciled: August 27, 2026

Primary market: Waxhaw, North Carolina and nearby southern Charlotte communities

Commercial focus: Landscaping and hardscaping

## Executive direction

Avanti should win by proving local project expertise, not by publishing a large set of lightly differentiated city pages. The first growth cycle should strengthen the existing landscaping and hardscaping hubs, add four high-intent service pages backed by real project evidence, and improve mobile performance. A dedicated Waxhaw page comes next; additional towns should be earned by demand and operating evidence.

The current audit baseline is 62/100 overall. Technical fundamentals are mostly sound, but organic growth is constrained by a 5.33-second mobile lab LCP, thin commercial copy, two blog URL/content mismatches, limited project and review proof, and inconsistent metadata.

## Positioning

Avanti's most defensible search position is:

> A local Waxhaw landscaping and hardscaping team that plans, builds, and maintains practical outdoor spaces, with visible project proof and clear service-area expertise.

This supports two primary customer needs:

- Property improvement: verified edging, stone, drainage, beds, sod, grading, and related outdoor improvements; patios, retaining walls, fire pits, lighting, and other expansion services remain candidates until the owner confirms current scope and supplies evidence.
- Ongoing confidence: clear process, local soil and drainage knowledge, real photos, realistic scope guidance, and accessible contact options.

### Business fact gate

The paver-patio and retaining-wall URLs in this plan are opportunity hypotheses, not permission to publish those service claims. Before either page enters production, the owner must confirm that Avanti currently offers the work, define scope and exclusions, and provide original project evidence. If that gate fails, replace the page with a verified service opportunity rather than weakening the standard.

## Priority page strategy

### Tier 1: strengthen existing commercial hubs

1. /services/landscaping
2. /services/hardscaping

Each parent page should explain who the service is for, local problems solved, process, material or design choices, realistic constraints, related services, project examples, and a clear next step. Preserve the existing self-canonical URLs and valid Service schema.

### Tier 2: build four high-intent service pages

Create these only when Avanti can supply truthful scope details and original project evidence:

1. /services/landscaping/drainage-grading
2. /services/landscaping/sod-installation
3. /services/hardscaping/paver-patios
4. /services/hardscaping/retaining-walls

These pages are more commercially useful than immediately expanding to many towns. Each should include service boundaries, process, variables that affect cost and timing, local site considerations, related project photos, FAQs in visible copy, and a direct estimate path.

### Tier 3: conditional expansion

Potential later pages include landscape lighting, plantings and softscapes, rock installation, fire pits, and walkways or outdoor steps. Create them only after Search Console or lead data shows demand and the business has enough unique evidence to avoid thin pages.

## Geographic strategy

Waxhaw is the first location-page priority. A useful Waxhaw page must contain locally specific service information, project examples, neighborhoods or site conditions that Avanti genuinely serves, and links to the relevant service pages.

Marvin and Weddington are the next candidates, but only after at least one of these signals exists:

- Search Console impressions or queries from the market
- Google Business Profile discovery or direction activity
- Completed jobs and original photos in that town
- Qualified lead volume that justifies a dedicated page

Do not launch a nine-city template set. Every indexed location page must be materially distinct and supported by operating evidence.

## Content and authority strategy

Publish two useful pieces per month. Prioritize problem-led and decision-led topics that support landscaping and hardscaping service pages. Every article should have:

- A concise answer near the top
- A named author or qualified reviewer
- Original Avanti project photos where possible
- Local context without keyword stuffing
- Links to one parent service, one specific service, and a relevant project
- Published and updated dates
- External citations only where factual claims require them

Case studies should become the primary proof asset. A strong case study documents the initial problem, site constraints, chosen solution, materials, process, outcome, location at an appropriate privacy level, and original images.

## Technical priorities

1. Improve the homepage mobile LCP from the measured 5.33 seconds to at most 2.5 seconds. The hero image is the current LCP element; reduce render delay and image waste without degrading quality.
2. Replace the two misleading blog URLs with durable descriptive slugs and one-hop 301 redirects:
   - /blog/spring-vs-fall-cleanup currently contains a rock-versus-mulch article.
   - /blog/core-aeration currently contains a sod-versus-seed article.
3. Replace request-time sitemap lastmod values with real content or deploy timestamps.
4. Shorten overlong titles and descriptions while preserving local relevance and service intent.
5. Keep schema aligned with visible facts. Existing LandscapingBusiness, Service, BlogPosting, and BreadcrumbList coverage is a sound base.
6. Treat FAQPage markup as optional validation, not a rich-result growth tactic; commercial sites generally should not expect FAQ rich results.
7. Review AI crawler policy intentionally. Blocking GPTBot is a separate licensing choice and does not by itself prove that ChatGPT search retrieval is blocked.

On August 27, PR #26 and its Railway build correction in PR #27 were verified live: the homepage displayed the 5.0/49 rating summary and three Google-supplied written review cards, while `/privacy` and `/terms` returned `200`.

## Main risks

- Scaling city pages before unique proof exists
- Making cost, warranty, certification, or service-area claims that the business has not verified
- Treating schema as a substitute for visible content
- Publishing generic articles without project evidence or internal links
- Declaring performance fixed from desktop results while mobile LCP remains slow
- Changing URLs without one-hop redirects, canonical updates, and internal-link cleanup

## Six-month outcome

By February 2027, Avanti should have two substantially stronger service hubs, four focused child services, one evidence-rich Waxhaw page, at least four case studies, twelve decision-useful articles, corrected URLs and sitemap dates, and a measured organic lead baseline. From months 7–12, the work shifts from page creation to authority, conversion quality, refreshes, and only evidence-approved expansion. Further service and geographic expansion should follow observed demand rather than a predetermined page count.
