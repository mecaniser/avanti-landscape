# Avanti Client SEO Tasks

Prepared: August 27, 2026

These actions require business knowledge, media rights, or access to a managed provider account. The development and SEO implementation can ship without them, but new service, project, and location pages remain gated until their inputs are approved.

## 1. Approve the business fact sheet

Confirm in writing:

- Services currently offered and work Avanti does not accept
- Cities, ZIP codes, and neighborhoods actively served
- Public hours and the correct phone number, `980-328-7141`
- Owner name, public role, relevant experience, and any credentials approved for publication
- Exact insurance, warranty, license, and certification language, or confirmation that it should be omitted
- Whether paver patios and retaining walls are currently offered, including scope boundaries

Do not send private account passwords or publish a private service-area-business address.

## 2. Supply project evidence

For each qualifying project, provide:

- Customer permission to publish the media and a privacy-safe location
- Customer objective and the visible problem
- Site, access, drainage, grade, or material constraints
- Approved scope and materials
- Outcome and maintenance guidance that Avanti can support
- Original before, during, and after photographs

The first requested set is one drainage or grading project, one sod project, and—only if the service fact gate passes—one paver or retaining-wall project.

## 3. Google Business Profile

- Verify the public phone, hours, primary/secondary categories, services, and city/ZIP-based service areas
- Remove any entire-state service-area entry and keep the private address hidden if customers are not served there
- Respond to current and future reviews through a policy-compliant process
- Add original project photos and short factual project updates regularly
- Record monthly review count, rating, response coverage, calls, and discovery activity

## 4. Google Search Console

- Export the dated clicks, impressions, queries, pages, devices, and geography baseline
- Inspect the Contact URL and confirm whether Google still shows the old `980-338-7141` snippet
- After the SEO release is live, inspect and request indexing for the two corrected blog URLs
- Submit the sitemap only after its live stability and URL set are verified

## 5. Google Analytics

- Mark the existing `generate_lead` event as the primary key event
- Review the new `click_to_call` and `click_to_text` events by landing page and link placement
- Define how a form submission, call, or text becomes a qualified lead and record that outcome outside GA4 without sending personal information to analytics

## 6. Local authority

- Identify legitimate suppliers, associations, partners, neighborhood organizations, and community projects that may publish a relevant Avanti mention
- Prefer real relationships and project references; do not buy links or participate in reciprocal link schemes

## Publication gates

Do not approve a new service, project, Waxhaw, Marvin, or Weddington page until the service is verified, scope and exclusions are approved, original evidence exists, the copy is materially unique, and its conversion and internal-link paths are defined.
