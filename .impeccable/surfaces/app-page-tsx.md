# Homepage — Property Plan

- **Mode and job:** Persuade homeowners in Waxhaw and nearby communities to see Avanti as the one owner-involved local crew for recurring lawn care, landscaping, light hardscape work, and maintenance. Quote and call/text are equal actions.
- **Approved composition:** `.impeccable/mocks/home-property-plan-guided-route.png` (approved 2026-08-13). Retained hero video is the living property field; the orange route maps four services into evidence below the fold.
- **Implementation grammar:** Deep field-green panels, survey-linen evidence fields, graphite contour lines, lichen service markers, and one stake-orange route. Barlow Condensed is the display face; Manrope carries body and controls. Thin technical seams, no card grid, no decorative glass.
- **Inventory:** Hero video/poster (existing raster); service imagery and crew/project imagery (existing raster); before/after slider (existing semantic component); route/map/markers/icons/contours/CTA and motion controls (semantic HTML, CSS, SVG). Hardscaping is represented only by the verified edging, stone, drainage, and bed evidence currently on hand.
- **Motion:** Video runs only where user preferences permit. The route draws once as a reading aid; service selection transitions immediately and is keyboard reachable. Reduced motion receives the full plot, static and visible.
