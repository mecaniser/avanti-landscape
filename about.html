About Avanti Landscaping

A local lawn care and landscaping team focused on quality, consistency, and honest service.

Our Story

Built On Attention To Detail

Avanti Landscaping got its start caring for lawns right here in Waxhaw, NC. We specialize in tall fescue and Bermuda lawns suited to our local climate, and we've grown by treating every yard — big or small — with the same level of care.

Today we offer lawn care, landscaping, hardscaping, and full property maintenance for homeowners, HOAs, and businesses across the greater Waxhaw area.
